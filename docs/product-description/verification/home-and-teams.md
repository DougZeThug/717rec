# Verification: home and teams

How to run this file: most of it is read-only, so run it signed out first and
sign in only for the rows that name a role. Three sections need real state you
cannot make from the browser — a **hidden team's name**, a **team with two
seasons played**, and a **season open for confirmation** — so get all three from
an admin before you start. `/teams` stores three display preferences and a scroll
position in the browser, so clear the site's storage before the
`browse-teams.md` section and again after it, or its first-visit defaults cannot
be seen. The `my-team.md` rows include real writes: **rename only a team you
own, and put the old name back**. Device values: `visitor`, `player`, `admin`,
`browser`, `phone`, `offline`, `slow`, `second browser`.

Not checkable by hand:

- Whether the database refuses a "Confirm your team" answer for a team the caller
  does not belong to. The card no longer offers one
  ([B-41](../bug-triage.md#b-41-the-confirm-your-team-card-has-no-sign-in-check-and-lists-hidden-teams)),
  but hiding a control and refusing a write are two different mechanisms, and the
  row-level policy on `season_participation` needs schema inspection.
- How noticeable the page's growth is in practice on a normal connection. HOME-18
  records that it grows; whether it is disruptive is a judgement.
- What an image card looks like when its picture fails to load rather than being
  absent. Needs a card whose address is deliberately broken.
- Whether blocks arriving are announced to a screen reader. They are not, by the
  document's own account; confirming it needs a real screen reader.
- How many league cards the league should be able to publish at once. Nothing
  limits it and they stack above everything else; that is a product decision.

  their own. The browser only ever offers their own, so the rule can only be
  reached outside the UI.

  that team's name. It needs a live match on the real league and a rename in the
  middle of it.

  another. They are not, by the document's own account; needs a real screen
  reader.

Not checkable by hand:

- Whether the standings' compare control is discoverable. It appears only on
  hover on a desktop row; whether a user finds it is a judgement, not a test.
- What the percentile badges show for a league with very few teams. Needs a
  league in that state.
- Whether the swap button is announced usefully. It carries an icon and no text;
  needs a real screen reader.
- Whether comparing career rather than season numbers is deliberate. The first
  block is headed "Career Statistics" and nothing offers a season view; that is a
  product decision for the league.
- Whether the head-to-head block's exclusion from the app's freshness rules is
  intended. COMPARE-30 measures how stale it gets; whether that is acceptable is
  a product question.
